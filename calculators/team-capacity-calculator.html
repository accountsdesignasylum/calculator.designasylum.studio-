How the math works. Each person's week is a fixed budget of hours, and every project spends from it. Add up what the team has, subtract what the projects take, and what is left is the only room you have for new work. A project marked "whole team" splits its hours evenly across everyone.

Why this beats gut feel. Saying yes usually happens because the team feels free this week. The budget view shows the hours already promised, so you find out you are out of room while you can still say no, instead of two weeks after you said yes.
