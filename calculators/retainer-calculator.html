How the math works. Take-home is the profit left after tax, so profit = retainer minus expense, and you keep 100% minus your tax rate of that. To hit a take-home target that is a share of the retainer, the rate you pay and the share you keep have to add up to less than 100%, otherwise no price works.

One caveat. This applies a flat rate to profit. Real Indian tax depends on structure: a proprietorship pays slab rates, a company pays corporate tax, and presumptive taxation under 44ADA changes the taxable base entirely. Use this to set a floor, not to file returns.
