How the math works. Total cost is your monthly salaries times the number of months, plus the other expenses. Profit is the quote minus that cost, and you save what is left of the profit after tax. To save a share of the quote, your tax rate and your savings share have to add up to less than 100%, otherwise no quote works.

One caveat. This applies a flat rate to profit. Real Indian tax depends on structure: a proprietorship pays slab rates, a company pays corporate tax, and presumptive taxation under 44ADA changes the taxable base entirely. GST is not included, so add it on top of the quote. Use this to set a floor, not to file returns.
